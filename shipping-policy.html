Shipping Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2025

Thank you for visiting and shopping at MoreTrendz. Following are the terms and conditions that constitute our Shipping Policy.

Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-3 business days. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or holidays.

If we are experiencing a high volume of orders, shipments may be delayed by a few days. Please allow additional days in transit for delivery. If there will be a significant delay in the shipment of your order, we will contact you via email or telephone.

Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates

Shipping charges for your order will be calculated and displayed at checkout.

Delivery estimates are as follows:

  • Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days
  • Express Shipping: 2-4 business days

Please note that delivery delays can occasionally occur and are outside of our control.

Shipment Confirmation & Order Tracking

You will receive a Shipment Confirmation email once your order has shipped, containing your tracking number(s). The tracking number will be active within 24 hours.

Damages

MoreTrendz is not liable for any products damaged or lost during shipping. If you received your order damaged, please contact the shipment carrier to file a claim.

Please save all packaging materials and damaged goods before filing a claim.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Shipping Policy, please contact us through our Contact Page.
